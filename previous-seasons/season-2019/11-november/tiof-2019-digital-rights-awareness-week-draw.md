# \[#TIOF] 2019 Digital Rights Awareness Week (DRAW)

## About

The IO Foundation, in collaboration with The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission, organized the 2019 Digital Rights Awareness Week (DRAW), a conference spanning over a whole week that included:

* 3 days workshops on Data-Centric Digital Rights
* 1 day of panels and conversations
* 2 days of training
* 1 day of private training for MCMC personnel
