## Why join this Internship?

TIOF Fellows represent The IO Foundation on an international stage while contributing to advancing the Data-Centric Digital Rights (DCDR) advocacy by actively engaging in Standards Developing Organizations (SDOs) with their communities and the technical standards they produce.

An internship in The IO Foundation is an opportunity to grow your career with a real environment combining advocacy and production. You will be mentored by experienced members of the organization and provided with all the tools you will need to carry out your duties during your internship.

For more information, make sure to check the [Terms of Reference](tiof-internship-cohort-q1-2026.md#terms-of-reference) ([Benefits](tiof-internship-cohort-q1-2026.md#benefits) + [Responsibilities](tiof-internship-cohort-q1-2026.md#responsibilities)).

## **Terms of Reference**

Applicants must understand and abide by the following:

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### **Requirements**

* [x] You can join the internship by two modalities based on your location of residence:

- In-person (for those who stay or plan to stay in Kuala Lumpur during the internship)
- Remote (for those who stay in any other part of the globe)

* [x] In-person interns will perform their duties at TIOF's HQ, unless otherwise specified.
* [x] Remote interns will perform their duties at their corresponding Argos Club, unless otherwise specified.
* [x] Ability to work independently and collaboratively in dynamic environments
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}


* [x] Passion for and commitment to The IO Foundation's [mission](https://tiof.click/TIOFMission) and [values](https://tiof.click/TIOFValues)
* [x] **Languages**

- English fluent both oral and written
- Fluent level of local official languages is a plus.

* [x] Strong networking skills and a proactive approach to building relationships within the tech community will be a plus.
* [x] Strong understanding of data privacy and technology issues will be a plus.
* [x] Proven experience in event participation, public speaking or advocacy will be a plus.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}



{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Responsibilities

By becoming an Intern you commit to the following responsibilities:

* [x] Actively participate in TIOF's operations, projects and activities.
* [x] Fulfill the duration and working hours of the internship, which will be agreed between TIOF and the intern, on the basis of the respective university's requirements.

{% hint style="success" %}
## NOTICE

The IO Foundation will give a final score based to Intern performance and will produced certificate when the Terms of Reference are fulfilled by both parties.
{% endhint %}
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
### &#x20;

* [x] Execute, diligently, the assigned tasks.
* [x] Actively participate in the corresponding Work Meetings and check-ins as needed.
* [x] Provide all administrative documents that are required by both TIOF and the respective university, as and when requested.
* [x] Collaborate with other TIOF Members to enhance the impact of the organization's advocacy effort.s.
* [x] Act as a responsibly and in accordance to [TIOF's Code of Conduct](https://short.theiofoundation.org/TIOFPolicyCoC)\
  Being a TIOF Intern implies representing The IO Foundation and effectively communicating our mission, values and initiatives.
{% endcolumn %}

  * [x] Access to exclusive training by TIOF

  * [x] Stay informed on current trends and developments in technology and data privacy to contribute to discussions and strategic planning.
  * [x] Participate in monthly TechUp Community meetings.
