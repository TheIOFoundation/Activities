{% columns %}

## About

The APNIC 61 conference will be held in Jakarta, Indonesia.

This conference is organized together with the APRICOT 2026 conference.

The IO Foundation will be there in-person to support the adoption and implementation of network practices that protect digital citizens through robust network design.

`Please visit the` [`[#APNOG] APRICOT 2026`](temp-apnog-apricot-2026.md) `page for all details about this conference.`
